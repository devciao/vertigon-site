Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Vertigon is a game made by an independent developer. This page explains what the game stores, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • A username you choose. It is shown on the world rankings. Please do not use your real name.
  • A random account ID. The game creates it automatically on first launch so your scores belong to you. There is no email, password, phone number or sign-in with another service.
  • Your game results. For each finished run: score, best combo, shape reached, accuracy, how long the run lasted and when it was played.

That is all. Vertigon does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, advertising identifier or device identifiers.

What we do not do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone for marketing.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Where the data is kept

Usernames and scores are stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union). Supabase processes the data only to run the game's rankings.

Your settings, personal statistics and daily streak are stored only on your device.

What other players can see

Your username and your scores appear on the rankings. Nothing else is visible to other players.

Deleting your data

Open Profile → Delete account in the game. This permanently removes your account, your username and all of your scores from our servers at once. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

You can also change your username at any time in Profile → edit.

Children

Vertigon does not ask for personal information and contains no ads, purchases or chat. It is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
